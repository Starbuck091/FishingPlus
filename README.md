FishingPlus mod for The Long Dark
by Starbuck097

FishingPlus is a mod for the game The Long Dark, designed to improve the existing fishing system in the game. 

New features:

- You can no longer fish with an empty hook, as it used to be. Imho, this is unrealistic. Now, in order for you to catch fish, you definitely need bait or bait, or both.

- The bait no longer increases the expectation. In vanilla, Lure increases the chance of rare fish, but lengthens the biting time. Now the bait either speeds up fishing, or at least does not slow it down.

- The dependence of biting on weather conditions. Now the intensity of fish biting in the game depends on the weather, time of day, wind strength and direction, as well as meteorological events such as aurora or electrostatic fog.

- Depletion of ice fishing holes. Now the fish in the hole is not infinite, and its quantity depends on meteorological conditions. From time to time, you will have to give the old fishing spots a few days to rest in order for the fish to return there again.

- Tying fish to baits and lures. Now every fish has a penchant for a certain type of bait/bait. For example, goldeye will not peck at meat baits, and predatory fish will not peck at corn.

- Expanding the range of baits. It is now possible to use some food items as bait for fishing, such as raw meat, animal fat, and some canned goods.
